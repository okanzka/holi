💡How to travel for less

📅Fly mid-week

Tuesday and Wednesday departures are often the cheapest. Friday and Sunday are usually the priciest.

🔀Flex by a day or two

Moving one day either side can change fares a lot. That's what the date grid is for.

🕵️Compare sites

Meta-search sites (Google, Skyscanner, Kayak) find different fares than agencies like Kiwi or Trip.com.

🏷️Write prices down

Type each fare you see into the planner. It ranks your real options for you.
