🧭 Quick guide

How to use heybe

Three pages, one trip. Here's how to get from "I need a holiday" to a booked flight and hotel.

🧳1. Plan your trip on Home

  1. Pick where from and where to

    Type a city, airport or code. Choose 🌍 Anywhere as the destination to see deals to every place from your airport.

  2. Set your dates and flexibility

    Pick rough dates, then ± 1–4 days. The more flexible you are, the more cheap combinations you'll see.

  3. Add travellers and cabin, then hit “Find my flights”

    Your trip is saved in the browser and shared with the Flights and Hotels pages, so you only type it once.

✈️2. Find a flight

  1. Check the live prices

    “Top picks” and “Best deals” show live fares. “Check every airline too” adds prices from airlines the cheap-fare feed misses.

  2. Use the fare calendar and date grid

    Each square is a departure/return pair. Green is cheaper. Click a square to open that exact date pair.

  3. Open the booking sites

    One click opens 8 booking sites with your route and dates already filled in. Compare and book where it's cheapest.

  4. Save the fares you see

    Write each price into “Fares you've saved”. Heybe ranks them so you can see your best real option.

🏨3. Find a hotel

  1. Your destination and dates are already filled in

    Change them if you want, set a budget per night and star rating, then hit “Find stays”.

  2. Pick a stay option

    Each square is a check-in/check-out pair. Fewer weekend nights usually means a lower price.

  3. Compare 7 hotel sites and keep a shortlist

    Open all sites at once, then save the hotels you like with their price and rating. Sort by cheapest, best value or highest rated.

💡Good to know

💾Saved on this device

Your trip, fares and shortlist stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to an account.

🔁Prices change

Live prices are a guide. Always check the final price on the booking site before you pay.

🗓️Mid-week is cheaper

Tuesday and Wednesday flights are often the cheapest. Try them in the date grid.

🏷️Start over anytime

Go back to Home and change your trip. Every page updates to match.
