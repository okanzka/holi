🧭 Quick guide

How to use heybe

Four pages, one trip. Here's how to get from "I need a holiday" to a booked flight and hotel.

🧳1. Plan your trip on Flights

  1. Choose the kind of trip

    Round trip or One way. 🔀 Multi-city builds home → city → city → home from direct flights. ? → Last stop → Home picks a cheap first city for you before a stop you choose; ? → Last stop → B does the same but ends somewhere else (B).

  2. Pick where from and where to

    Type a city, airport, country or code, in English, Turkish or German. Choose 🌍 Anywhere on either side to see deals to (or from) every place.

  3. Set your dates and flexibility

    Pick rough dates, then ± 1–4 days. The more flexible you are, the more cheap combinations you'll see.

  4. Narrow it down (optional)

    🛂 Visa keeps only places your Turkish passport can go to the way you want (visa-free, Schengen, e-visa…). ✈️ Airlines, Direct flights only and Also search nearby airports do what they say. Trip budget adds a stay to each Anywhere deal, so you see flight + hotel as one total.

  5. Add travellers and cabin, then hit “Search”

    Your trip is saved in the browser and shared with the Hotels and Things to do pages, so you only type it once.

✈️2. Find a flight

  1. Check the prices

    “Top picks” and “Best deals” show live Ryanair fares plus recent fares from other airlines (Pegasus, Wizz Air, AJet, Lufthansa…). “Check every airline too” opens sites that search every airline live.

  2. Use the fare calendar and date grid

    The calendar shows the lowest fare per day in a week or month view. In the date grid each square is a departure/return pair; green is cheaper. Click a square to open that exact date pair.

  3. Open the booking sites

    One click opens every booking site with your route and dates already filled in. Compare and book where it's cheapest.

  4. Save the fares you see

    Write each price into “Fares you've saved”. Heybe ranks them so you can see your best real option.

  5. Plan the stays with 🏨

    Press 🏨 on any flight option. You go city by city with hostel and hotel suggestions with real prices, pick one per city, and get the total for stays + flights.

🏨3. Find a hotel

  1. Your destination and dates are already filled in

    Change them if you want, set a budget per night and star rating, then hit “Find stays”.

  2. Pick a stay option

    Each square is a check-in/check-out pair. Fewer weekend nights usually means a lower price.

  3. Compare 7 hotel sites and keep a shortlist

    Open all sites at once, then save the hotels you like with their price and rating. Sort by cheapest, best value or highest rated.

🎟️4. Things to do

  1. Your cities are already there

    Every city of your trip and stay plan gets its top 10 sights, most famous first. Add any other city by name.

  2. Read more or find it on the map

    Each place links to Wikipedia and Google Maps, and every city has a travel guide link.

💡Good to know

💾Saved on this device

Your trip, fares and shortlist stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to an account.

🔁Prices change

Live prices are a guide. Always check the final price on the booking site before you pay.

🗓️Mid-week is cheaper

Tuesday and Wednesday flights are often the cheapest. Try them in the date grid.

🏷️Start over anytime

Go back to Flights and change your trip. Every page updates to match.

📱On your phone

The menu shows icons: ✈️ Flights, 🏨 Hotels, 🎟️ Things to do, 🧭 this guide. The sun/moon button switches dark mode.

🛂Check visa rules

The visa list is for Turkish passports as of Nov 2026. Rules change, so confirm with the embassy before you travel.
