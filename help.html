🧭 Quick guide

How to use heybe

Flights, buses, trains, hotels and sights for one trip. Here's how to get from "I need a holiday" to a booked trip.

🧳1. Plan your trip on Flights

  1. Choose the kind of trip

    Round trip or One way. 🔀 Multi-city builds home → city → city → home from direct flights. ? → Last stop → Home picks a cheap first city for you before a stop you choose; ? → Last stop → B does the same but ends somewhere else (B). 👥 Meet up and ✈️ My flights are explained below.

  2. Pick where from and where to

    Type a city, airport, country or code, in English, Turkish or German. Choose 🌍 Anywhere on either side to see deals to (or from) every place.

  3. Set your dates and flexibility

    Pick rough dates, then ± 1–4 days. The more flexible you are, the more cheap combinations you'll see.

  4. Narrow it down (optional)

    🛂 Visa keeps only places your Turkish passport can go to the way you want (visa-free, Schengen, e-visa…). ✈️ Airlines, Direct flights only and Also search nearby airports do what they say. Trip budget adds a stay to each Anywhere deal, so you see flight + hotel as one total.

  5. Add travellers and cabin, then hit “Search”

    Your trip is saved in the browser and shared with the Hotels and Things to do pages, so you only type it once.

  6. Every tab keeps its own search

    Switch between Round trip, Multi-city, Meet up and the rest: each tab remembers what you typed there, and a tab you haven't used yet starts with the defaults. Travellers, cabin, currency and filters stay the same on every tab.

✈️2. Find a flight

  1. Check the prices

    “Top picks” and “Best deals” show live Ryanair fares plus recent fares from other airlines (Pegasus, Wizz Air, AJet, Lufthansa…). “Check every airline too” opens sites that search every airline live.

  2. Use the fare calendar and date grid

    The calendar shows the lowest fare per day in a week or month view. In the date grid each square is a departure/return pair; green is cheaper. Click a square to open that exact date pair.

  3. Open the booking sites

    One click opens every booking site with your route and dates already filled in. Compare and book where it's cheapest.

  4. Save the fares you see

    Write each price into “Fares you've saved”. Heybe ranks them so you can see your best real option.

  5. Plan the stays with 🏨

    Press 🏨 on any flight option. You go city by city with hostel and hotel suggestions with real prices, pick one per city, and get the total for stays + flights.

👥3. Meet a friend from another city

  1. Open the 👥 Meet up tab

    Put your city in You fly from and your friend's in Friend flies from. In Meet in pick a city, or 🌍 Anywhere to find the cheapest places you can both reach with a direct flight.

  2. Same days first

    Plans where you both arrive and fly home on the same days come first. With ± 1 or ± 2 days you also see plans where one of you arrives or leaves a day or two apart, e.g. “From Berlin: arrive 1 day later”.

  3. See who pays what

    Every plan shows the total for all 4 flights and what each city pays, e.g. “Nuremberg: €57 · Berlin: €76”, with a Book link for each flight.

✈️4. Pick your own flights

  1. Open the ✈️ My flights tab

    Add the direct flights you want, in order: e.g. Nuremberg → Palma, then Málaga → Nuremberg. They don't have to connect: cover the gap by train or bus.

  2. Give the dates

    First flight around and Last flight around set the trip; the flights between can be on any day. Give a flight its own day if it must be around then, and set the nights between two flights.

  3. Get the best packages

    You get up to three: Cheapest, Best balance and Closest to your days, each with the nights in each place. A gap shows “🚆 by train or bus” with links to the Buses and Trains pages.

🚌5. Go by bus or train

  1. 🚌 Buses: live FlixBus prices

    Type the cities (you see the flag and country), choose One way or Round trip, the date and ± days. The cheapest-per-day strip finds the best day; a round trip shows the total both ways. Choose 🌍 Anywhere in To to see the cheapest places FlixBus goes from your city.

  2. 🚆 Trains: times and changes across Europe

    Type the stations, the date and the time you want to leave after; a round trip also lists the trains back. Train companies don't share prices, so Check price opens Deutsche Bahn or the company's own site. FlixTrain prices are shown live below the timetable.

  3. 🌍 Anywhere by train

    Shows the places FlixTrain goes from your station with their usual price. Open one to see every train that day.

🏨6. Find a hotel

  1. Your destination and dates are already filled in

    Change them if you want, set a budget per night and star rating, then hit “Find stays”.

  2. Pick a stay option

    Each square is a check-in/check-out pair. Fewer weekend nights usually means a lower price.

  3. Compare 7 hotel sites and keep a shortlist

    Open all sites at once, then save the hotels you like with their price and rating. Sort by cheapest, best value or highest rated.

🎟️7. Things to do

  1. Your cities are already there

    Every city of your trip and stay plan gets its top 10 sights, most famous first. Add any other city by name.

  2. Read more or find it on the map

    Each place links to Wikipedia and Google Maps, and every city has a travel guide link.

💡Good to know

💾Saved on this device

Your trip, fares and shortlist stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to an account.

🔁Prices change

Live prices (Ryanair, FlixBus) are a guide. Always check the final price on the booking site before you pay.

🗓️Mid-week is cheaper

Tuesday and Wednesday flights are often the cheapest. Try them in the date grid.

🏷️Start over anytime

Go back to Flights and change your trip. Every page updates to match.

📱On your phone

The menu shows icons: ✈️ Flights, 🚌 Buses, 🚆 Trains, 🏨 Hotels, 🎟️ Things to do, 🧭 this guide. The sun/moon button switches dark mode. On iPhone, open the site in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen to get it as an app.

🛂Check visa rules

The visa list is for Turkish passports as of Nov 2026. Rules change, so confirm with the embassy before you travel.
